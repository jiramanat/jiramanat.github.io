Introduction

Have you ever picked up a Rubik's Cube and had no idea what to do with it? If so, here's a guide on how to master the 3x3 Rubik's Cube.

The Cube

- There are six colored sides: White, yellow, green, blue, red, and orange.

- Center pieces: Pieces with one color located in the center of each face.

- Edge Pieces: Pieces with two colors located in between the corner pieces.

- Corner Pieces: Pieces with three colors located at each corner of the cube.

-

Move Notations

Each algorithim uses standard notation to list each move required. Each move is represented using capital letters to tell which face to turn.

- R (right) -L (left)

-U (up/top) -D (down/bottom)

-F (front) B (back)

- Apostrophe ('): Turn the face counter-clockwise

- 2: Turn the face twice
