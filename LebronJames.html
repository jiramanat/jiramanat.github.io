"King James"

Beginnings

Lebron was born in Akron, Ohio, and he attended St.Vincent-St. Mary High School

He immediately became recognized by scouts across the country, and was even featured on the cover of Sports Illustrated.

NBA Career

Lebron has played 23 seasons in the NBA, in the teams of the Cleveland Cavaliers, Miami Heat, and Los Angeles.Currently, he is about to start his 24th season with the Philadelphia 76ers.

Lebron holds the record for the most points of all-time, and his accumulated many accomplishments over the years, such as his 4 NBA championships, 4 MVPs, 1 rookie of the year, his 22x NBA All-Star selections.

Summary

Lebron is considered one of the greatest to play the game of basketball, and has cemented himself as a household name in sports.
